Context notes

A compact field guide

DES

A 64-bit-block Feistel cipher with 16 rounds, substitution boxes, permutations, and a reversible round-key schedule.

3DES EDE

Encrypt with K1, decrypt with K2, then encrypt with K3. Decryption reverses that sequence: K3, K2, K1.

Legacy baseline

ECB and 3DES are shown for academic continuity. ECB reveals repeated-block patterns and neither choice is recommended for modern sensitive data.
